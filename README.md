# Educator Commons

Status: v0.1 draft · Formative educational resources · Not piloted

Educator Commons is the Trivian Institute's practical entry point for teaching across the Educational Commons. It gives educators, librarians, community facilitators, and independent group leaders session plans, adaptation guidance, and ways to notice how learners reason. The materials are free to read and adapt. They require neither a live AI account nor technical background.

## Begin with a session

| If you have | Start here | Learners leave with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 60 minutes | [Interaction Literacy session](sessions/interaction-literacy-60-min.md) | An interaction map and a bounded request |
| 60 minutes | [Casebook session](sessions/casebook-60-min.md) | A reasoned decision and a revision after new information |
| 15 minutes of planning | [Session planner](planning/session-planner.md) | A runnable plan with access and consent choices |

Use [Start Here](START_HERE.md) to choose a source and [the adaptation guide](adaptation/access-and-choice.md) to make participation possible without disclosure, movement, public speech, or live AI access. [Reasoning evidence](assessment/reasoning-evidence.md) shows what to observe without scoring agreement.

## Across the Commons

- [Interaction Literacy](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/interaction-literacy): concepts, lessons, and exercises.
- [Relational AI Casebook](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/relational-ai-casebook): fictional dilemmas and facilitation notes.
- [Embodiment Commons](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/embodiment-commons): optional attention and environment practices.
- [Youth Interaction Literacy](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/youth-interaction-literacy): age-responsive activities with adult facilitation.
- [Learning in Practice](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/learning-in-practice): careful, non-identifying accounts of teaching and revision.

These repositories contain educational resources. A session plan is not an effectiveness claim, professional credential, or license to deploy technical systems. The paid Ahava Way Assisted Stretch Certification is outside this Commons.

## Contribute and status

The plans await facilitator, accessibility, and learner feedback. See [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md), [community expectations](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), and [editorial status](EDITORIAL_STATUS.md). Contact **learn@trivianinstitute.org**. For reporting what happens in a session, begin with [Learning in Practice](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/learning-in-practice) and its privacy boundaries.
