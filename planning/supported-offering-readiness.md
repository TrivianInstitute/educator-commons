# Before offering a supported learning experience

Status: proposal for human review; not an authorization to sell or a report of a pilot.

The educational commons remains available to read, adapt and teach under its existing
licenses. A supported offering can charge for preparation, facilitation, organized delivery
or feedback while clearly identifying the free source materials and their reuse rights.

## Describe the learner's actual experience

Record the audience, prerequisites, edition, source links and what the learner receives.
Separate available materials from planned recordings, future support or untested activities.
Do not describe an outline as a finished course. State the scope and limits of any formative
assessment; completion is not professional certification or proof of effectiveness.

For each included resource, record its creator, license, source revision, adaptations and
required notices. Institutional hosting does not establish an intellectual-property assignment.
Do not include unpublished manuscripts, restricted certification curricula, third-party media
or private learner material without a separate rights review.

## Try the complete path before inviting payment

- Ask a first-time learner to find the prerequisites, open the materials, complete the core
  activity and explain what they learned. Record obstacles and support minutes.
- Check keyboard access, readable layout, alternatives to personal disclosure, and usable
  formats for the intended audience. Provide transcripts for any required recordings.
- Define success before the pilot. Report all participants, non-completions and limitations;
  a small usability pilot does not validate educational effectiveness.
- Verify that the described support can be delivered by a named responsible person within
  stated boundaries. Avoid a promise of unlimited founder or facilitator availability.
- Have the authorized operations owner verify purchase confirmation, delivery, repeat access
  and exception handling separately. A working lesson does not establish working commerce.

## Keep participation voluntary

Do not require marketing consent, public testimonials, personal narratives or research
participation to access a purchased educational service. Keep optional follow-on offers
distinct from the instructional task. A learner may disagree, decline bodily practices or
choose an accessible alternative without being treated as less successful.

Do not publish identifiable learner work or pilot records in this repository. For public
teaching accounts, follow [Learning in Practice](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/learning-in-practice)
and its privacy and permission process. The [session planner](session-planner.md) supports
planning; the [adaptation guidance](../adaptation/access-and-choice.md) supports access choices.

## Human release decision

An authorized reviewer confirms content, rights, accessibility, truthful scope, support
capacity and the operational delivery checks. Pricing, publication, financial activation,
refund policy, contributor agreements and institutional commitments require their own
appropriate approvals. This checklist grants none of those authorities.
